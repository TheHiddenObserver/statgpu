## Findings closed in this pass

1. **CRITICAL — public covariance non-finite input fail-closed contract.**
   `clustered_covariance`, `two_way_clustered_covariance`, `hac_covariance`, and
   `driscoll_kraay_covariance` reject non-finite `X`/residual arrays before
   signed/grouped reductions. NumPy, maintained Torch CPU, and physical CuPy/Torch
   runner coverage lock this API boundary.

2. **HIGH — projection-created dynamic-range backend coverage.**
   Two-way demeaning refreshes its stability classification after the first entity
   projection. Maintained Torch CPU and physical CuPy/Torch fixtures prove the
   safe-raw to risky-post-projection transition.

3. **CRITICAL — order-dependent multi-tier two-way covariance reduction.**
   A third-magnitude nonnested covariance could become `Inf` only after preceding
   Torch covariance tests because signed grouped components were reduced inside
   BLAS before Cameron-Gelbach-Miller cancellation. The unsafe multi-tier branch
   now keeps grouped row products separate, decomposes group-debias factors into
   exact powers of two, and uses compensated estimator-level summation. Gram-safe
   multi-tier and ordinary single-tier paths remain vectorized.

4. **MEDIUM — precision fallback scope/performance.**
   Row-level compensated covariance accumulation is restricted to the pathological
   unsafe branch; Gram-safe multi-tier covariance stays on vectorized BLAS/GPU
   products.

5. **CRITICAL — fixed-effect recovery could reintroduce unstable group means.**
   Post-fit one-way maps had fallen back to raw scatter means and two-way recovery
   could miss projection-created cancellation risk. Effect recovery now reuses the
   shared stable group means on uncentered level residuals and certifies transformed
   residuals before accepting convergence. Strict cancellation-tail regressions are
   preserved.

6. **CRITICAL — two-way effect normalization could overflow before cancellation.**
   Repeated `time_effect * group_count` normalization could overflow even when the
   observation-weighted shift was finite. The normal-scale path defers the common
   shift until convergence and computes it with `stable_mean(time_effects[t_idx])`.

7. **CRITICAL — two-way common Gram scaling could erase a recoverable product.**
   A grouped component can survive common score scaling while a mathematically
   nonzero self/cross product underflows before inclusion-exclusion. A 2x2
   nonnested `[-1e308, 1e-100, 1e308, 1e-100]` fixture has a representable low-order
   CGM remainder. The unsupported common-Gram product range now fails closed with
   `FloatingPointError` rather than silently publishing zero.

8. **CRITICAL — centered fixed-effect maps could overflow although predictions were
   finite.**
   Fixed-effect maps are stored directly on the level-residual scale. `_grand_mean`
   remains for the diagnostic contract but `PanelOLS.predict()` no longer constructs
   an unrepresentable centered effect. NumPy/Torch and physical CuPy/Torch coverage
   include the 1-vs-10 `+/-1e308` case.

9. **MEDIUM — obsolete fixed-effect prediction mask.**
   Once level-scale maps removed grand-mean restoration, the extra host-side known-
   label mask became dead O(n) work and was removed.

10. **CRITICAL — pooling-F regressor centering could lose cancellation tails.**
    The pooled-null `X` column means used an overflow-only reduction while the rest
    of panel diagnostics had moved to the magnitude-tiered mean. The helper now
    reuses the shared stable mean. `[1e308, 1, -1e308, 0, 0, 0]` retains the exact
    `1/6` column mean on NumPy and maintained Torch CPU, with the same physical
    CuPy/Torch audit.

11. **CRITICAL — sparse two-way incidence graphs could choose an out-of-range FE
    gauge.**
    Additive entity/time effects have a common-shift null direction. The historical
    weighted-zero gauge can drive an individual compact effect beyond DBL_MAX even
    when the observed levels and an equivalent decomposition are finite. Near the
    float64 boundary only, recovery now range-centers `[entity, -time]` at its
    midrange during ALS, minimizing the stored infinity norm; ordinary-scale
    recovery keeps the historical weighted-zero gauge. A connected sparse graph at
    `5e307` scale passes direct NumPy/Torch recovery and public `fit -> predict`,
    while the existing `2**50 +/- 1` low-order regression remains unchanged.

12. **CRITICAL — delayed covariance scale restoration had a range-unsafe
    multiplication association.**
    `(covariance * large) * small` can transiently overflow although the final
    product is finite; always multiplying the small factor first has the symmetric
    underflow failure. Restoration now composes covariance, projection, and design
    factors in mantissa/exponent form, renormalizes the combined mantissa, then
    restores the final exponent in bounded power-of-two chunks. Complementing
    `1e200`/`1e-200` coordinate scales and cross-stage `1e200`/`1e-100` scales are
    covered on NumPy, maintained Torch CPU, and the physical CuPy/Torch runner.

13. **CRITICAL — stable means did not make physical `value - mean` centering safe.**
    With one `+1e308` observation and ten `-1e308` observations, the grand mean is
    finite but the positive centered value is about `1.818e308`, outside float64.
    Pooling F, constant-only classical F, parameter R-squared, and adjusted
    R-squared now switch to a common dimensionless response working scale only when
    physical centering would overflow; ordinary-scale subtraction is untouched.
    The hard gate verifies the exact-fit results `R²=1`, adjusted `R²=1`,
    `F=Inf`, `p=0` on NumPy/Torch and registers the pooling-F case for physical
    CuPy/Torch validation. The previous overflow/invalid-matmul warnings disappear.

14. **MEDIUM — stale physical-artifact documentation.**
    Existing P100 artifacts are explicitly historical reference evidence. They
    predate the latest shared-reduction, FE-gauge, covariance-restoration, and
    diagnostic-centering changes and cannot serve as exact-head physical
    acceptance.
